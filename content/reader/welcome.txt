Welcome to the Zero2W Reader.

This application demonstrates reusable application controls through the launcher.

Press KEY_1 to move to the next page.

Short-press KEY_ENTER to move to the previous page.

Hold KEY_ENTER to close Reader and return to the application menu.

Reader progress is saved automatically, so the same page will reopen the next time Reader starts.

Additional plain-text documents can be copied into the content/reader directory.
